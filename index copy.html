MY PASSPORT

More than places,
they’re tastes worth remembering.

I love traveling, but if I’m being honest, food is usually a big part of why I want to go. I get excited about trying the things I’ve only seen online, heard people rave about, or wondered what they actually taste like.

So this website is basically a little collection of the places I’ve been and, of course, the food I got to eat along the way. Some dishes became instant favorites, some were just fun to try, and some were so good I’m still thinking about them. Because for me, a trip just feels a little better when there’s good food involved.

ABOUT THE TRAVELER

Hi, I'm Alyannah.

I’m a nursing student who loves traveling, trying new things, and, honestly, documenting almost everything along the way. I also really love eating—it’s probably one of my favorite ways to de-stress. I enjoy taking photos and videos because it’s one of the ways I express myself. I like capturing the little things—the food, the scenery, the people, and even the random moments that probably wouldn’t seem important to anyone else.

I guess that’s also why I love traveling. There’s always something new to see, experience, eat, and capture.
