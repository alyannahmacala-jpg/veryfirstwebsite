Thailand

A flavorful journey that gave me even more reasons to come back.

MY JOURNEY

A Taste Of Thailand

Thailand might have to be one of my favorite countries when it comes to food trips. I went there for the experience, but honestly, the food was a huge part of what made the trip memorable. I found myself craving Thai food even after leaving—especially the satay and mango sticky rice.

There was something about the flavors, the variety, and the way every meal felt so satisfying that made me want to come back. If I had the chance, I’d definitely return to Thailand—not just to explore more, but for another food trip.

THE BITES

My Top 3 Favorite Foods

A few of the flavors that made my Thailand trip even more memorable.

03

Mango Sticky Rice

This was probably my biggest food obsession in Thailand. The sweet mango, sticky rice, and especially that creamy coconut ice cream were just too good. I loved it so much that I actually brought three packs home. Yes, three. That should tell you everything.

02

Chicken Satay

I don't think I can go to Thailand without getting satay. The grilled chicken was flavorful on its own, but that peanut sauce made it even better. Simple, but honestly one of those things I could keep eating without getting tired of it.

01

Thai Milk Tea

And of course, I had to try the real Thai milk tea. It was creamy, sweet, and had that distinct Thai tea flavor that makes it so addictive. After having it there, regular milk tea just doesn't hit the same.

MY REFLECTION

Why I’d Go Back for the Food

Thailand was one of those trips where I came home still thinking about the food. I loved how bold and comforting the flavors were, and I honestly didn't expect to crave them this much afterward.

I think that's what made the trip special for me. Some places leave you with beautiful pictures, but Thailand left me with cravings—and a pretty good reason to go back.
