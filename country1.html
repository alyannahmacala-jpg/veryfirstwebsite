DESTINATION 01

Japan

A long-awaited journey into a culture I had always been curious about.

MY JOURNEY

A Taste Of Japan

Japan was definitely an interesting experience, especially seeing how different the culture is from what I’m used to in the Philippines. I really appreciated how disciplined and respectful people were, although I think I’d still prefer the warmth and community we have back home.

And honestly, I can see why people keep coming back. Between the peaceful surroundings, beautiful scenery, and, of course, the food, Japan definitely lived up to the hype.

THE BITES

My Top 3 Favorite Foods

A few of the flavors that made my Japan trip even more memorable.

03

Wagyu Ramen

This might have to be my top 1. The wagyu beef was so tender, juicy, and flavorful—I honestly couldn't get enough of it. One bite and you'll understand why I kept coming back for more.

02

Matcha

As a certified matcha lover, I had matcha every single day in Japan. I had to savor the chance—when else would I get to enjoy authentic matcha in Japan?

01

Japanese Yakiniku

Japan’s take on grilled meat—and of course, I had to try it! It reminded me a lot of samgyupsal, except this one was halal, making it both familiar and exciting at the same time.

MY REFLECTION

Why I Understand the Hype Now

Japan had always been a place I wanted to experience for myself, and being there was completely different from seeing it through a screen. What stood out to me was the discipline, respect, and sense of personal space—so different from the Philippines, but fascinating in its own way.

And when it came to food, it was nice to try something completely different from the usual Southeast Asian flavors I’m more familiar with. The yakiniku and matcha were definitely highlights, and I loved getting to experience food that felt so different from what I normally eat.
