DESTINATION 03

Malaysia

A flavorful journey that gave me even more reasons to come back.

MY JOURNEY

A Taste Of Malaysia

Malaysia was a pretty comfortable place for me to travel, especially because it’s a Muslim country and finding halal food wasn’t something I had to worry much about. I could just look at the menu, order, and enjoy without overthinking it.

The only problem? The spice. A lot of the food was way spicier than what I’m used to, and as someone with a pretty low tolerance for spicy food, that definitely made some meals a little harder to enjoy. Still, I had fun trying different dishes and experiencing Malaysia’s food culture—and I’d definitely be willing to go back and try more, preferably with less chili this time.

THE BITES

My Top 3 Favorite Foods

A few of the flavors that made my Malaysia trip even more memorable.

03

3-Layer Tea

This was easily my favorite! It was so creamy, sweet, and just really good. I loved it so much that when I got home, I actually bought Melacca gula so I could try making it myself.

02

Nasi Lemak

I really wanted to love this without struggling with the spice. The flavors were so good, but my low spice tolerance definitely had me reaching for water every few bites. Still worth trying—and suffering a little for.

01

Laksa Noodles

The laksa was definitely an experience. It had so much flavor and that rich, comforting noodle-soup thing going on. I just had to be careful with the spice because, apparently, Malaysia wanted to test my tolerance.

MY REFLECTION

The Spice Was Worth It

Malaysia was definitely an interesting food trip for me. I learned pretty quickly that my spice tolerance and Malaysian food have very different ideas of what “mild” means. But despite that, I still enjoyed the experience and would be curious to try more dishes next time.

And thankfully, I don’t have to fly all the way back to Malaysia whenever I get a craving. I found a restaurant in Manila that serves some of the dishes I tried there. It’s still a bit of a trip from where I am, but hey, Manila is definitely closer than Malaysia.
